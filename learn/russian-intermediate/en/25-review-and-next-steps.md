### 2. The ТРКИ system: levels and subtests

**ТРКИ** (Тест по русскому языку как иностранному; in English often TORFL) is the Russian state system of tests in Russian as a foreign language. It has six levels, which roughly correspond to the European CEFR levels:

| Level | Full name | CEFR | In plain words |
|---|---|---|---|
| ТЭУ | элементарный уровень | A1 | survival Russian |
| ТБУ | базовый уровень | A2 | simple everyday communication |
| ТРКИ-1 | первый сертификационный уровень | B1 | independent everyday and study communication |
| ТРКИ-2 | второй сертификационный уровень | B2 | confident communication, including professional topics |
| ТРКИ-3 | третий сертификационный уровень | C1 | high, near-professional level |
| ТРКИ-4 | четвёртый сертификационный уровень | C2 | level close to an educated native speaker |

After the Beginner book you are around A2; after this book you have covered the grammar of B1 and much of B2. ТРКИ-1 is a realistic first goal; ТРКИ-2 is a good goal for the end of your first or second PhD year.

The exam consists of five **subtests**:

| Subtest | What it tests | How to prepare |
|---|---|---|
| Лексика. Грамматика | cases, aspect, verbs of motion, participles, word choice | do exercises from this book again, quickly and without the key |
| Чтение | understanding texts: main idea and details | read news and popular science every day; answer "who, what, why" |
| Аудирование | understanding spoken Russian: dialogues, announcements, short talks | podcasts, lectures, news; listen twice — first for the main idea, then for details |
| Письмо | writing a text for a purpose: a letter, a message, a short summary | write one short letter or summary a week and have it corrected |
| Говорение | dialogues in situations, telling, giving your opinion | speak with classmates only in Russian; record yourself |

Requirements, formats and practical details change over time and differ between testing centres, so always check the current information with the centre where you plan to take the exam and with your university. Official sample tests (образцы тестов) are published for each level; practise with them under real time limits.

### 3. Useful phrases for speaking and writing

In the Говорение and Письмо subtests you often have to give your opinion and structure a short text. These phrases help you sound organized:

**Giving an opinion:**
- Я считаю, что… · По-моему, … · Мне кажется, что… — *I think… · In my opinion… · It seems to me…*

**Structuring:**
- Во-первых, … Во-вторых, … В-третьих, … — *Firstly… Secondly… Thirdly…*
- Например, … — *For example…*
- С одной стороны, …, с другой стороны, … — *On the one hand…, on the other hand…*

**Concluding:**
- Таким образом, … · Подводя итог, … · В заключение хочу сказать, что… — *Thus… · Summing up… · In conclusion I want to say that…*

**Gaining time (speaking):**
- Это интересный вопрос. · Дайте подумать. · Как бы это сказать… — *That is an interesting question. · Let me think. · How can I put it…*

Write the hyphen in во-первых, во-вторых, в-третьих — it is part of the spelling.

## Study tip

After this book, the best "textbook" is your own field. Read one Russian article or abstract on LLMs a week and write down ten new terms. Give your next seminar talk from Russian notes, not English ones. Write emails to Ivan Petrovich and to the dean's office in Russian, even when English would be faster. Keep your vocabulary in one place — a notebook or flashcards — with stress marks and the case each word takes (на основе + gen., по сравнению с + instr.). And speak: language you only read stays passive. Удачи на экзамене! (*Good luck in the exam!*)
