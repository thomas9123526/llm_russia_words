# Lesson 5. Who Is This? What Is This? Это…, Noun Gender, Personal Pronouns

> **Russian for Beginners** · Level: A1 · **You will learn:** to ask Кто э́то? and Что э́то? and to answer with Э́то… · the three genders of Russian nouns · the personal pronouns я, ты, он, она́, оно́, мы, вы, они́

This lesson gives you the first real piece of Russian grammar. You will learn the most useful sentence for a beginner — **Э́то…** (*This is…*) — and the two questions that go with it: **Кто э́то?** (*Who is this?*) and **Что э́то?** (*What is this?*). Then you meet an idea that is new for speakers of Korean and English: **grammatical gender**. Every Russian noun is masculine, feminine or neuter, and you can almost always see the gender from the last letter of the word. Finally, you learn the personal pronouns and how **он** and **она́** can mean *it*.

In the dialogue Dima looks at the photos on Minho's laptop and at the things on his desk.

## Dialogue

**Ди́ма:** Минхо́, кто э́то?  
Minkhó, kto éto?  
*Minho, who's this?*

**Минхо́:** Э́то Ива́н Петро́вич. Он профе́ссор.  
Éto Iván Petróvich. On proféssor.  
*That's Ivan Petrovich. He's a professor.*

**Ди́ма:** А э́то кто?  
A éto kto?  
*And who's this?*

**Минхо́:** Э́то А́нна. Она́ аспира́нтка.  
Éto Ánna. Oná aspirántka.  
*That's Anna. She's a PhD student.*

**Ди́ма:** А что э́то?  
A chto éto?  
*And what's this?*

**Минхо́:** Э́то статья́.  
Éto stat'yá.  
*It's a paper.*

**Ди́ма:** А э́то? Слова́рь?  
A éto? Slovár'?  
*And this? A dictionary?*

**Минхо́:** Нет, э́то не слова́рь. Э́то кни́га.  
Net, éto ne slovár'. Éto kníga.  
*No, it isn't a dictionary. It's a book.*

**Ди́ма:** А где слова́рь?  
A gde slovár'?  
*So where's the dictionary?*

**Минхо́:** Вот он.  
Vot on.  
*Here it is.*

**Ди́ма:** А ру́чка?  
A rúchka?  
*And the pen?*

**Минхо́:** Вот она́.  
Vot oná.  
*Here it is.*

**Ди́ма:** А письмо́?  
A pis'mó?  
*And the letter?*

**Минхо́:** Вот оно́.  
Vot onó.  
*Here it is.*

## Grammar

### 1. Э́то… — "This is…"

**Э́то** points at someone or something: *this is, that is, it is, these are*. It never changes, and Russian needs no verb "is" and no articles (*a*, *the*):

- Э́то кни́га. — *This is a book. / That's a book.*
- Э́то Ива́н Петро́вич. — *This is Ivan Petrovich.*
- Э́то моде́ль. — *It's a model.*

To ask a question you have two question words:

- **Кто э́то?** — *Who is this?* — for **people and animals**.
- **Что э́то?** — *What is this?* — for **things and ideas**.

Note that Russian uses **кто** also for animals: **Кто э́то? — Э́то кот.**

A **yes/no question** looks exactly like a statement; only the voice changes. Raise your voice sharply on the word you are asking about: **Э́то слова́рь?** (*Is this a dictionary?*) — the voice jumps up on **-ва́-** and falls at once. To answer **no**, put **не** (*not*) before the word you deny:

- Кто э́то? — Э́то А́нна. — *Who's this? — It's Anna.*
- Кто э́то? — Э́то кот. — *Who's this? — It's a cat.*
- Что э́то? — Э́то то́кен. — *What's this? — It's a token.*
- Э́то слова́рь? — Да, э́то слова́рь. — *Is this a dictionary? — Yes, it is.*
- Э́то слова́рь? — Нет, э́то не слова́рь. Э́то кни́га. — *Is this a dictionary? — No, it isn't. It's a book.*
- Э́то профе́ссор? — Нет, э́то не профе́ссор. Э́то аспира́нт. — *Is this a professor? — No, it isn't. It's a PhD student.*

### 2. Noun gender: masculine, feminine, neuter

Every Russian noun has a **gender**: masculine (**м.**), feminine (**ж.**) or neuter (**ср.**). For people, gender usually follows sex (**студе́нт** is a man, **студе́нтка** a woman). For things it is purely grammatical: **стол** (*table*) is masculine, **кни́га** (*book*) is feminine, **окно́** (*window*) is neuter. Gender matters because other words must agree with the noun — the pronouns in this lesson, adjectives in Lesson 9, past-tense verbs in Lesson 14.

The good news: you can see the gender from the **ending** (the last letter) of the noun.

| Gender | Ending | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | consonant | стол, текст, то́кен, ноутбу́к, профе́ссор |
| masculine | **-й** | музе́й (*museum*), трамва́й |
| masculine | **-ь** (some) | слова́рь, день |
| feminine | **-а** | кни́га, ру́чка, ко́мната, А́нна |
| feminine | **-я** | статья́, неде́ля (*week*) |
| feminine | **-ь** (some) | моде́ль, нейросе́ть, тетра́дь (*notebook*) |
| neuter | **-о** | окно́, письмо́, сло́во (*word*) |
| neuter | **-е** | мо́ре (*sea*), зада́ние, обуче́ние |

Three things to remember:

1. **Nouns in -ь** can be masculine or feminine. There is no reliable rule, so learn them with their gender. In word lists this book always marks it: **слова́рь (м.)**, **моде́ль (ж.)**.
2. **Men's words in -а / -я** are masculine because they mean men: **па́па** (*dad*), **Ди́ма**, **Ва́ня**, **мужчи́на** (*man*).
3. A few borrowed words in **-о, -е, -и, -у** never change and are neuter: **метро́**, **кафе́**, **ра́дио**.

Many words for people have a masculine and a feminine form:

| Masculine | Feminine | |
|---|---|---|
| студе́нт | студе́нтка | student |
| аспира́нт | аспира́нтка | PhD student |
| коре́ец | корея́нка | Korean (person) |

- Минхо́ — аспира́нт, а А́нна — аспира́нтка. — *Minho is a PhD student (m.), and Anna is a PhD student (f.).*
- Минхо́ — коре́ец. — *Minho is Korean.*
- Э́то обуче́ние, а э́то зада́ние. — *This is training, and this is the task.*

### 3. Personal pronouns

| | Singular | | Plural | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st person | **я** | I | **мы** | we |
| 2nd person | **ты** | you (informal) | **вы** | you (polite or plural) |
| 3rd person | **он** / **она́** / **оно́** | he / she / it | **они́** | they |

**Я** is written with a small letter, unlike English *I*. In letters and emails, **Вы** is often written with a capital letter when it is addressed politely to one person.

In the third person the pronoun follows the **gender of the noun**, not the idea of "person or thing". So a masculine thing is **он**, a feminine thing is **она́**, a neuter thing is **оно́** — all three can be translated *it*:

| Noun | Pronoun | |
|---|---|---|
| слова́рь, ноутбу́к, стол (м.) | **он** | Где слова́рь? — Вот **он**. |
| ру́чка, моде́ль, статья́ (ж.) | **она́** | Где моде́ль? — Вот **она́**. |
| письмо́, окно́, зада́ние (ср.) | **оно́** | Где письмо́? — Вот **оно́**. |

The word **где** (*where*) asks for a place. The simplest answers are **Вот он / она́ / оно́** (*here it is*), **тут** (*here*) and **там** (*there*).

- Где ноутбу́к? — Вот он. — *Where's the laptop? — Here it is.*
- Где нейросе́ть? — Вот она́. — *Where's the neural network? — Here it is.*
- Где окно́? — Вот оно́. — *Where's the window? — There it is.*
- Где А́нна? — Она́ там. — *Where's Anna? — She's over there.*
- Где Ива́н Петро́вич? — Он тут. — *Where's Ivan Petrovich? — He's here.*
- Я аспира́нт, и ты аспира́нт. Мы аспира́нты. — *I'm a PhD student, and you're a PhD student. We're PhD students.*

*(аспира́нты is the plural — Lesson 6.)*

### 4. Э́то or он / она́?

Beginners often mix up **э́то** and the pronouns. They do different jobs:

- **Э́то** *introduces* or *names* something for the first time: **Э́то А́нна.** — *This is Anna.* It answers the questions **Кто э́то?** and **Что э́то?**
- **Он / она́ / оно́** *refer back* to something already mentioned and say something new about it: **Она́ аспира́нтка.** — *She is a PhD student.*

So in the dialogue Minho first says **Э́то А́нна** (who is in the photo) and then **Она́ аспира́нтка** (what she is). Compare:

| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| naming a thing: Что э́то? | **Э́то** кни́га. (not ~~Она́ кни́га.~~) |
| saying something about Dima | Э́то Ди́ма. **Он** аспира́нт. |

In short: **э́то** = *pointing*, **он / она́ / оно́** = *talking about*. Notice also that **э́то** never changes for gender or number — **Э́то А́нна, э́то Ди́ма, э́то письмо́** — while the pronoun must match the noun.

- Кто э́то? — Э́то Ива́н Петро́вич. Он нау́чный руководи́тель. — *Who's this? — It's Ivan Petrovich. He's the supervisor.*
- Что э́то? — Э́то нейросе́ть. Она́ тут. — *What's this? — It's a neural network. It's here.*

## Study tip

**Learn nouns with он, она́ or оно́.** Do not learn just **моде́ль = model**; learn **моде́ль — она́**. Say the word and the pronoun together: *стол — он, кни́га — она́, окно́ — оно́, слова́рь — он, нейросе́ть — она́*. This tiny habit will save you many mistakes with adjectives and the past tense later.

## Review

- You can ask **Кто э́то?** and **Что э́то?** and answer **Э́то А́нна. Э́то статья́.**
- You can say no: **Нет, э́то не слова́рь. Э́то кни́га.**
- You can find the gender of a noun from its ending: consonant/**-й** → м., **-а/-я** → ж., **-о/-е** → ср., **-ь** → learn it.
- You know all personal pronouns: **я, ты, он, она́, оно́, мы, вы, они́**.
- You can ask where something is and answer: **Где моде́ль? — Вот она́.**
