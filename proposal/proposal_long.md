## Motivation

## Research interests

I am interested in several well-known challenges that are widely discussed in the research community. The first is reliability: LLMs can produce fluent text that is factually incorrect, and methods such as retrieval-augmented generation are being studied to reduce this problem. The second is efficiency: training and deploying large models requires substantial computing resources, which motivates research on techniques such as quantisation, parameter-efficient fine-tuning and efficient inference. The third is multilingual quality: most LLMs are trained mainly on English data, and their performance in other languages, including Russian, can still be improved. The fourth is evaluation: it remains difficult to measure the true abilities and limitations of LLMs in a consistent and reliable way. I do not intend to fix my research question yet, as I would prefer to choose it together with you after studying the current literature.

I would also like to learn how to identify a meaningful research problem, design rigorous experiments and interpret their results carefully. In addition, I hope to improve my academic writing and learn how to present research results at conferences and in journals.
