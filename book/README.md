The order follows the [LLM Learning Roadmap](../LLM_Learning_Roadmap.docx) and the *Language AI Handbook*.

## How to study one chapter

1. **Read the Korean version** to understand the idea fully.
2. **Read the Russian version** with the English version open beside it. Look up sentences you don't understand in the English.
3. **Learn the Russian chapter's word list (Словарь главы) and phrases (Полезные фразы).** The phrases are sentence patterns that repeat in every technical text.
4. **Do the exercises.** Try to answer at least one of them in Russian.

The Russian in Parts 1–2 uses short, simple sentences. It gets gradually more complex, and from Part 6 it's normal academic Russian.

For more terms, see [LLM_Russian_Glossary.docx](../LLM_Russian_Glossary.docx) (with classroom phrases and the alphabet) and [docs/words/LLM_Russian_Glossary_Full.docx](../docs/words/LLM_Russian_Glossary_Full.docx) (1,195 terms).
